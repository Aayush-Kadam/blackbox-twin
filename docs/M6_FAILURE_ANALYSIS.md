# M6 failure analysis

The frozen campaign retains all 216 cells, including unsuccessful and unsupported ones.
There were no unexpected crashed/failed training cells. A budget stop can still yield an
inadequate earlier model; status=completed means the adapter finished its configured work,
not that behavioral reconstruction succeeded.

## Finite rare and delayed behavior

All highest-budget rare models failed finite-product equivalence. Their median trace
fidelity was .8 despite .9954 output fidelity: directed challenges exposed the rare event
that random ordinary sequences mostly missed. Increasing ordinary random testing did not
guarantee access to the required ordered path. Delayed depth variants similarly exposed
longer histories that learned models conflated. Only 6/27 delayed seed/method instances
were equivalent at the upper checkpoint. No strategy dominated all families.

The exact historical rare target and known delayed depth-5 structure are explicitly
labelled regression data. New variants are small controlled configurations, not a random
sample of industrial programs. No arbitrary-program equivalence claim follows.

## Wallet coverage versus accuracy

The fixed grid often makes incorrect concrete claims on unseen values, including one
configuration's false positive acceptance. Adaptive boundary probing reduces that error
in this bounded campaign. Hardened consensus inference also avoids concrete errors here,
but keeps ambiguous regions unresolved. Unknown transitions invalidate inferred state,
so one refusal may lead to many later refusals. This explains why long-trace coverage is
more demanding than one-step acceptance precision.

At 3000, hardened had lower trace fidelity than adaptive on five of six target means and
tied one; its median unseen-long fidelity was .55 versus adaptive .70. The same upper
and lower budget scores show that simply permitting more queries did not solve selection
and representation limits under the frozen probe schedule. No after-the-fact tuning was
performed. Explanations about better future policies are hypotheses, not measured wins.

The high overall wallet trace score also reflects many short exhaustive diagnostic cases.
These contain training-word intersections. They must not replace untouched random
unseen-long results when describing generalization. All intersections were reported.

## Inventory transfer

Inventory exposes independently varying available and reserved counters, outside the
existing balance/frozen guarded grammar. Adaptive and hardened adapters reject this
model class before training: 24 compatibility cells, zero original queries. This is a
representation gap, not a learned accurate inventory twin.

The generic finite-concrete learner spent its 500/1500 action budgets without completing
a hypothesis in all 12 runs. Each scored artifact is an explicit all-refusing fallback.
There is no hidden use of the evaluator's transition specification in the learned model.
Finite encodability does not imply practical learnability at the tested budget. Larger
budgets, richer representations or improved algorithms would require a new protocol.

## Reliability history and remaining limits

The original nine-cell pilot exposed a regression-budget handling defect: truncated
verification could be labelled completed. Before source freezing, the adapter was fixed
to propagate budget exhaustion and stop, then rechecked at exactly 1500 actions with
45/45 completed regressions. Both original pilot and corrected recheck are retained.

Two preimplementation baseline runs each had 129 passes / one Windows reliability failure:
a temporary-directory cleanup race and a process termination timeout. Focused recovery
checks passed, and the unchanged tests passed in the final full suite. These historical
failures were not deleted, weakened or represented as successful baseline runs.

Limits remain: three/two learner seeds; few small target variants; bounded integer domains
and declared update grammar; no learned schema discovery; no unbounded data structures,
identifiers/references, time, nondeterminism or third-party programs. Validation cases
check the returned state fields, but do not independently prove every future continuation
is state-preserving after malformed input. Peak memory is unmeasured. Training isolation
is an architectural/data-access check, not a hostile-code security boundary.

M7 may demonstrate only these measured scopes and must expose abstention, unknown state,
budget exhaustion, compatibility rejection and lack of equivalence prominently. The
readiness verdict permits a future decision; M7 work has not begun.
